MEDIAPRONET is a curated discovery platform for newly launched startups, SaaS tools and indie products. Its motto: discover what founders just shipped.

## Features

- Every submission passes an automated content check before it is published
- Each product gets a profile page with description, category, tech stack, screenshots, user reviews and a link to the live site
- Browse by category, tag, tech stack ("built with"), the AI tool used to build a product, or launch month
- Community votes feed a daily and weekly ranking
- "Where to promote your startup" guide: 212 places with their submission rules and costs, plus a four-week launch plan
- Machine-readable: `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, RSS feeds and a sitemap

## Free tier

Browsing is free. Makers can list a product for free (shown with the MEDIAPRONET badge on their site, published after at least 7 days), or pay once from $19 to go live instantly. Submit: https://mediapronet.com/submit
