# Easypanel

Easypanel provides a browser dashboard for deploying and managing applications on your own server. Connect GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket repositories, deploy Docker images and Docker Compose services, and manage databases alongside your applications.

It includes automatic SSL, resource monitoring, logs and a browser shell. One-click templates are available for tools such as n8n, Supabase, WordPress, Immich and Nextcloud.

# Free plan

- Up to 3 projects.
- Unlimited services and deployments.
- Basic monitoring.
- Free forever; you provide your own server.

Easypanel is proprietary software with a free plan and optional paid subscriptions. Server hosting costs are separate. See the [official pricing page](https://easypanel.io/pricing) for current limits and paid features.
