SitePatent is a discovery platform for startups, tools and online products: the directory for builders who ship.

## Features

- Every submission passes an automated content check before it is published
- Each product gets a permanent page with description, category, tech stack, screenshots, user reviews and a link to the live site
- Browse by 47 categories, tags, tech stack ("built with"), the AI tool used to build a product, or launch month
- Trending products, alternatives and compare pages
- Machine-readable: `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` expose the full listing index

## Free tier

Browsing is free. Makers can list a product for free (permanent listing, published after at least 7 days with the SitePatent badge on their homepage), or pay once from $19 to go live instantly. Submit: https://sitepatent.com/submit
